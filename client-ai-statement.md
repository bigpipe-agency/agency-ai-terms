We use AI tools in parts of the work we do for you. This note sets out where, how a person checks the work, and how your data and your rights are protected. You can ask us about any of it at any time.

## Where we use AI

We use AI tools for:

- research, analysis and summarizing material
- first drafts of copy and content, which our team then rewrites and edits
- generating or editing images and design elements
- generating or editing video, voice and audio
- producing ad variations and optimizing campaigns
- preparing reports and analyzing campaign data
- writing code for websites and automations
- AI agents that carry out agreed tasks on your accounts, such as scheduling posts, adjusting bids or sending messages

## A person checks every deliverable

Apart from the agent tasks described below, nothing made with AI reaches you, or your audience, until a senior member of our team has reviewed it, edited it where needed and approved it. That review covers facts, sources and claims, and whether the work fits your brand. AI makes us faster; it does not replace the judgment you hired us for.

## Your data

We do not put your confidential information or any personal data you share with us into an AI tool without your written consent.

> Alternative if you use business AI tools that do not train on client data: "We put your confidential information only into AI tools we use under business terms that stop the provider from training its models on it. We do not put personal data you share with us into any AI tool without your written consent. Ask us at any time for the list of tools we use."

## AI-generated images, video and voice

We tell you whenever an image, video, voice or audio file we deliver was generated with AI, and we use only tools whose terms allow commercial use of what they produce. We never imitate a real person's likeness or voice without their written permission. Nothing of this kind is published or placed before you approve it.

> Keep only if you generate images, video, voice or audio.

## AI agents on your accounts

Where an AI agent carries out tasks on your accounts, it works only within limits we agree with you in writing: what it may do, how often and with what budget. A person monitors it, and we stop it as soon as you ask.

> Keep only if AI agents act on client accounts (publishing, bids, messages).

## Ownership

Work made with AI is yours on the same terms as any other deliverable under our agreement. One thing to know: copyright protects what a person authors. In the U.S., the Copyright Office has said that prompts alone are not enough, so material an AI tool generates on its own may not be protected. That is one more reason a person shapes and edits everything we deliver.

## Fees

Our fees are set by deliverable and scope, as agreed in each statement of work, not by the hours the work takes. Using AI where it helps is part of how we deliver that standard.

> Optional. Keep this section only if you price by deliverable or scope rather than by the hour.

## Your policy comes first

If your company has its own rules for how suppliers may use AI, send them to us and we will follow them, agreeing with you any change they make to scope or fees. If anything here does not work for you, tell us and we will agree a different approach in writing.
