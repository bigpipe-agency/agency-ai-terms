This addendum forms part of the agreement between [Agency name] ("Agency") and [Client name] ("Client") (the "Agreement"). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Agreement.

**1. Definitions.** "AI Tools" means software that generates or modifies text, images, audio, video, code or analysis from prompts or data, including large language models and image, video and voice generators. "AI-Assisted Deliverable" means any Deliverable created in whole or in part with AI Tools.

**2. Permitted use.** Agency may use AI Tools in performing the Services for the following purposes: (a) research, analysis and summarization; (b) drafting copy and content; (c) generating or editing images and design elements; (d) generating or editing video, voice and audio; (e) producing advertising variations and optimizing campaigns; (f) preparing reports and analyzing performance data; (g) writing software code for websites and automations; and (h) operating AI agents that take actions on Client's accounts or systems. Agency will not use AI Tools for any other purpose under this Agreement without Client's prior written consent, which may be given by email.

**3. Human review.** Before any AI-Assisted Deliverable is delivered to Client or published, a senior member of Agency's team will review it, edit it as needed and approve it, including checking its factual claims and sources and its conformity with Client's brand guidelines. Actions taken within the limits set under the Autonomous actions clause are governed by that clause.

**4. Confidential information and personal data.** Agency will not input Client's Confidential Information, or any personal data provided by or on behalf of Client, into any AI Tool without Client's prior written consent.

> Alternative clause 4 if you use business AI tools that do not train on client data: Agency will input Client's Confidential Information only into AI Tools provided under business or enterprise terms that prohibit the provider from using inputs or outputs to train or improve its models and that bind the provider to confidentiality. Agency will not input personal data provided by or on behalf of Client into any AI Tool without Client's prior written consent. On request, Agency will give Client a list of the AI Tools it uses for the Services.

**5. Generated media.** Agency will identify to Client each Deliverable that contains images, video, voice or audio generated with AI Tools, and will use for that purpose only AI Tools whose terms permit commercial use of their outputs. Agency will not use AI Tools to imitate the likeness or voice of an identifiable real person without that person's written permission. Agency will not publish or place any such Deliverable without Client's prior approval.

> Keep only if you generate images, video, voice or audio.

**6. Autonomous actions.** Where Agency uses AI Tools that take actions on Client's accounts or systems without a person approving each action (for example, publishing content, changing bids or budgets, or sending messages), Agency will do so only within scope, frequency and spending limits agreed with Client in writing, will monitor those actions, and will stop them promptly on Client's request.

> Keep only if AI agents act on Client accounts without a person approving each action.

**7. Ownership.** Subject to payment of the applicable fees, ownership of AI-Assisted Deliverables passes to Client on the same terms as other Deliverables under this Agreement. Client acknowledges that material generated by AI Tools without sufficient human authorship may not be protected by copyright in some jurisdictions, and Agency gives no warranty that such material is capable of copyright protection. Agency warrants that it uses AI Tools in accordance with their terms of use.

**8. Fees.** Fees for the Services are set by deliverable and scope as stated in the applicable Statement of Work. Agency's use of AI Tools in performing the Services does not by itself change the agreed fees.

> Optional. Keep only if you price by deliverable or scope.

**9. Client AI policy.** Agency will comply with any written policy on the use of AI by suppliers that Client provides, once the parties have agreed in writing any change to scope or fees that the policy requires.

**10. Liability.** Each party's liability arising from AI-Assisted Deliverables is subject to the limitations and exclusions of liability in this Agreement.
