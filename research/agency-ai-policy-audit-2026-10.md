# Agency AI policy audit, October 2026

How many small marketing agencies say in public how they use AI? We read the websites of 104 of them on October 19, 2026.

| What the website shows | Agencies | Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mentions AI anywhere on the homepage | 38 of 104 | 37% |
| Presents AI as part of what it sells | 29 of 104 | 28% |
| Says in writing how it uses AI (policy page or a section of its privacy policy or terms) | 6 of 104 | 6% |
| Publishes a dedicated AI policy page | 2 of 104 | 2% |
| Sells AI and also says how it uses it | 4 of 29 | 14% |

## Method

- **Sample.** 197 company websites of founders of advertising and marketing services companies with 2 to 10 employees in the US and UK, drawn from a professional-profile database in October 2026. One had no website; 44 of the other 196 could not be read (expired, parked, not responding, or no text without a browser); 48 of the 152 readable sites are not client-service agencies (software, media owners, networking groups, events, shops, schools, one French-language site, one agency whose terms forbid automated access). That leaves 104 agencies: 83 in the US, 21 in the UK.
- **Pages read.** The homepage, its same-site links, the XML sitemap, nine common policy addresses (such as /ai-policy), the about page and up to four legal pages.
- **Definitions.** A policy page is a page about the agency's own use of AI with at least one commitment. A legal-page section is a headed section of a privacy policy or terms on how the agency uses AI in its services. "Mentions AI" means the homepage text contains AI, artificial intelligence, generative AI, ChatGPT or LLM. "Sells AI" means the homepage presents AI as part of the agency's services, judged by reading each mention. Every positive was confirmed by reading the page.
- **Limits.** One database, very small agencies, so it describes small agencies rather than the industry. The rates are floors: a statement in a proposal or behind a login is not counted. Agencies are named only where they publish a statement.
